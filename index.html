Department of Physics · IIT Kanpur

From quarks to colliding stars

We are faculty, postdoctoral fellows and graduate students in the Department of Physics at IIT Kanpur. Our work spans particle theory, collider and neutrino experiments, and string theory — from the particles made at the LHC to dark matter, the early universe, black holes and the gravitational waves of merging stars — and many of our projects cross between them.

Particle Theory

Physics beyond the Standard Model, the early universe and the structure of the proton.

Beyond the Standard Model · Effective field theory · Phase transitions & gravitational waves · Dark matter & cosmology

Particle Experiments

New-particle searches with CMS, neutrino experiments and machine learning for particle physics.

Dark matter & new-particle searches · Machine learning & explainable AI · Jet substructure & tagging · Neutrino oscillations (DUNE)

String Theory

Holography, black holes, Carrollian physics and quantum systems out of equilibrium.
